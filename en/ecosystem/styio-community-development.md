# styio-community Governance and Maintenance Guide

This page describes the purpose and maintenance model of the `styio-community` repository.

`styio-community` is not a code repository. It is the deliberation space for the Styio ecosystem and the storage location for community rules.

## Core Responsibilities

`styio-community` is responsible for:

- **RFC process (Request for Comments)**: major language design changes and ecosystem architecture changes must be proposed here and discussed by the community.
- **Governance structure**: defines the rights, responsibilities, and promotion paths for the Core Team, Maintainers, and Contributors.
- **Code of Conduct**: communication baseline that all community members must follow.
- **Public events and meeting notes**: records technical steering committee meeting minutes and decisions.

## Difference from Primary Design Documents

- `styio/docs/design/` stores accepted and frozen language designs. It is the SSOT.
- `styio-community/rfcs/` stores proposals that are under discussion or historically processed.
- Once an RFC is accepted, concrete engineering specifications must land in the corresponding code repository, and the RFC is then archived.

## Maintenance Principles

1. **Transparent decisions**: major technical direction must not be decided in private chat. Every major decision needs a public RFC discussion trace.
2. **Consensus-driven closure**: use clear voting and resolution mechanisms to end technical disputes and avoid endless argument.
